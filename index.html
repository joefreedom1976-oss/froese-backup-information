Froese Website Backups

A private backup utility maintained by the owner of the Froese Family Research Center. It creates encrypted recovery copies of the website and associated files in the owner's Google Drive. It is not offered as a public service.

Privacy policy · Use of this utility

Privacy policy

Google Drive authorization is used to upload encrypted backups, download them to verify recovery, list backup files, and remove older backups according to the configured retention policy. The backup program is configured to operate in its designated backup folder.

Backup files are encrypted locally before upload. The recovery password is stored separately and is not uploaded with the archives. Google stores the encrypted files in the owner's Drive account. Google authorization credentials are stored locally with restricted access and are used only to operate this backup connection.

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Access to Google user data is used only to provide the backup and recovery functions described here.

The planned retention is ten daily recovery points and four weekly recovery points. The owner can stop backups and revoke this application's access through Google Account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing archives; the owner controls those files.

Questions about this private utility should be directed to its owner using the support email shown on Google's authorization screen.

Use of this utility

This utility is for its owner's personal use. Recovery depends on retaining the recovery password, available backups, and successful verification. This information page does not indicate that a backup has succeeded; consult the backup status and maintenance manual.
